## How to initialize this file

1. Open the official program page through an authenticated, approved channel.
2. Record the canonical policy URL and submission channel above.
3. Copy or accurately summarize the current scope, exclusions, testing rules,
   disclosure rules, safe-harbor terms, and reward table.
4. Record the synchronization date and preserve links to every authoritative
   source.
5. Review the live policy again before each new network or runtime phase.

Do not treat search-engine snippets, cached copies, or third-party summaries as
the contract.

## Essential rules

Replace or extend this section with the program's exact requirements. Until
then, apply these conservative defaults:

- Use only explicitly in-scope assets and your own accounts and test data.
- Never access another person's account or data without explicit authorization.
- Avoid privacy violations, destructive actions, service interruption, and
  service degradation.
- Stop if proof would require post-exploitation, persistence, data modification,
  or data destruction.
- Do not retain unnecessary personal, customer, player, or production data.
- Keep request volume, duration, and effects bounded.
- Keep the finding confidential and use the designated submission channel.
